\begin{abstract}
Track 2 asks for the removal of transient noise events from Indian-language speech, scored by SI-SDR on synthetic
mixtures plus the reduction in word error rate of the mandated SraVaani recogniser on all clips. We submit a single
audio-only pipeline applied identically to every clip: a fixed ensemble of four enhancement architectures
(MP-SENet, URGENT BSRNN, TF-GridNet and TF-Locoformer; seven checkpoints) with eight-view test-time augmentation.
All networks are fine-tuned with an SI-SDR loss plus a recognition (RNNT) loss through the frozen SraVaani model, on
mixtures of released and leakage-filtered public Vaani speech with reused validation noise events. The largest late
gains came from averaging \emph{diverse} TF-Locoformer members rather than training one model longer. The final
system scores 19.8688 Combined on the Phase~2 test set (SI-SDR 16.29\,dB, pooled $\Delta$WER 3.58).
\end{abstract}

\section{Training Data and Preprocessing}
\textbf{Released data.} We use the released Track~2 training clean/noisy pairs, holding out two Indian states for
validation, the released natural clips (only for a preservation loss), and the released validation set.

\textbf{Reused validation noise (``d32'' bank).} From the released validation pairs we extract 518 noise-event
recordings as noisy-minus-clean residuals. The organizers confirmed that training on validation noise is allowed.
The bank is used for training only; inference uses no noise recordings.

\textbf{Public Vaani speech (additional data).} We add 6{,}069 clean utterances from the public Vaani
corpus~\cite{vaani}. To prevent test leakage, we build an audio landmark-fingerprint index of all 3{,}178 Phase~2
test inputs, drop every Vaani recording matching a test input with $\geq 12$ votes, drop all recordings of any speaker
with $\geq 20$ matches, and exclude the two held-out states. Targets are pause-gated to match the convention of the
organizers' clean references. The organizers confirmed that public Vaani training is allowed.

\textbf{Augmentation.} Every trainer remixes on the fly: clean speech plus d32 events (placed at $+9\pm3$\,dB
event-to-speech level until $\sim$34\% of the clip is covered), released training residuals, or the clip's own
noise, followed by peak normalisation to 0.95.

\section{Proposed Method}
\textbf{Architectures and pretrained models.} (i) MP-SENet~\cite{mpsenet}, magnitude/phase enhancement, from the
public DNS checkpoint; (ii) BSRNN from the URGENT challenge baseline~\cite{urgent}; (iii) TF-GridNet~\cite{tfgridnet},
from the URGENT baseline; (iv) TF-Locoformer~\cite{locoformer}, from MERL's public DNS checkpoint. SraVaani-1.0
(ARTPARK-IISc, Hugging Face) is used frozen for the recognition loss and for transcription.

\textbf{Loss.} On synthetic mixtures
$\mathcal{L} = 2\,(-\mathrm{SI\text{-}SDR}(\hat s, s)) + 3\,\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{RNNT}}(\hat s, y)$, where
$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{RNNT}}$ passes the enhanced waveform through SraVaani's frozen encoder and joint network
with the released transcript $y$. On natural clips (no clean reference) we use
$0.2\,\|\hat s - \tilde s\|_1/\|\tilde s\|_1$ against a frozen teacher output $\tilde s$, which prevents the
enhancer from damaging real recordings. Optimiser: AdamW, gradient clipping at 3.

\textbf{Training strategy.} Each network is first adapted on released data, then on d32 remixes and on the
leakage-filtered Vaani speech. Continuation rounds of 40k steps at learning rate $5\times10^{-6}$ (single
H100/H200 GPU each) refined the TF-Locoformer and TF-GridNet. The final TF-Locoformer slot averages four checkpoints
from \emph{different lineages}: the scaled Vaani recipe (60k steps plus four continuation rounds), a released-data
recipe with natural preservation continued to 33k steps, and two reused-noise specialists (d32-only mixtures)
initialised from different points of the first lineage.

\textbf{Inference.} The ensemble is fixed:
$b = 0.3\,\mathrm{MP} + 0.7\,g\,\mathrm{BSRNN}$ ($g$: least-squares gain between the two outputs),
$c = 0.3\,b + 0.7\,\mathrm{TFGridNet}$, and
$\hat s = 0.3\,c + 0.7\,\overline{\mathrm{Loco}}$, where $\overline{\mathrm{Loco}}$ is the equal average of the four
peak-normalised TF-Locoformer outputs. BSRNN input is level-normalised to RMS 0.10. Each network runs on eight views
(polarity $\pm1$ $\times$ shifts of 0, 40, 80, 120 samples), which are inverted and averaged. Every clip receives
identical processing: no routing, passthrough, noise dictionary or template search.

Table~\ref{tab:official} lists official Phase~2 scores (Combined = SI-SDR + pooled $\Delta$WER; pooled
$\Delta$WER $\approx 0.35\,\Delta$WER$_{\mathrm{syn}} + 0.65\,\Delta$WER$_{\mathrm{nat}}$). The final system
reaches \textbf{19.8688}.

\textbf{Key findings.} (1) \emph{Diversity beats depth.} Adding differently-trained TF-Locoformer members gave the
largest late gain (+0.100 for F13), while continuing to train existing members gave smaller, eventually negative
returns (F14). (2) \emph{Only SI-backed gains transfer.} Candidates whose local gain was purely a few fewer ASR
errors (F14, F16) scored lower officially; candidates that also improved hard-clip SI-SDR transferred. (3)
\emph{Recognition-specific objectives did not transfer.} A matched TDT loss, RNNT weights of $10\times$ and
$25\times$, an artifact-boosted SDR loss, teacher labels, a generative Schr\"odinger-bridge enhancer and
second-stage denoisers were all neutral or negative under the exact package. (4) \emph{Where the remaining error
is.} On held-out synthetic clips our output leaves 615 SraVaani word errors against 507 for the clean reference;
71\% of that gap is substitutions (residual distortion), not deletions. (5) \emph{Evaluate the shipped package.}
A cascade that looked $+0.45$ pooled-$\Delta$WER better in a no-TTA harness gave nothing once evaluated
package-to-package with test-time augmentation.

\textbf{Conclusion.} A compliant, uniform neural ensemble reached 19.87 Combined, 2nd-best SI-SDR among the top
teams. Its main limitation is synthetic $\Delta$WER: the recogniser still substitutes words on our enhanced
speech. Closing that gap appears to need enhancement objectives that track recognition more directly than the
surrogate losses we tested.
